# Probing Question Bank

Claude pulls from this when you drop a signal. Not every question every time. Pick the 5–8 that would most change the verdict.

## About the signal itself
- Where exactly did you see it? (Platform, account, ad vs organic)
- When did you first notice it, and have you seen it again since? Rising or same?
- What's the hard number? Views, sales, ad count, reviews, price.
- Was the engagement real buying intent ("link?", "where do I get this") or just entertainment?
- Who was posting it? A brand, a creator, an affiliate, a random person?
- Have you seen it from more than one seller? How many?

## About the buyer
- Picture the person who buys this. Age, situation, what they were doing right before they bought.
- Are they buying it for themselves or as a gift? For whom?
- What were they using or doing before this existed?
- What would make them buy two?

## About the "why now"
- Why is this moving *now* and not last year?
- Is this a trend (months), a fad (weeks), or a shift (years)?
- What would kill the trend? A platform change, a news event, the season ending?

## About competition
- Who's selling it best right now, and what's their angle?
- What do the worst reviews of the current sellers complain about?
- Is there an angle or audience nobody's taken yet?

## About us
- Could we get this delivered before the moment it's for?
- Do we have an unfair advantage here? (Audience, creator access, supplier, niche knowledge)
- Would you be proud to run this, or embarrassed?
- If this works, what's the second and third product in the same store?

## Creative pressure tests
- Describe the first 3 seconds of the ad. If you can't, that's a signal.
- What's the "gift reframe" version of this?
- What's the premium version? The budget version?
- What does this product's existence make people need next? (Picks and shovels)
